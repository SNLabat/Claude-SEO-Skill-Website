The Three Layers of Modern Search

SEO vs GEO vs AEO — What's the difference?

Search has evolved beyond the blue link. To win today, you need to optimize for all three layers.

SEO

Search Engine Optimization

Goal: Rank on the first page of Google & Bing.

Optimizing for: Crawlers, algorithms, and ranking signals.

Key signals: Technical health, backlinks, page speed, content relevance, E-E-A-T.

Outcome: Your page appears when users search relevant keywords.

GEO

Generative Engine Optimization

Goal: Be cited or recommended by AI search (Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity).

Optimizing for: AI training data, retrieval systems, source authority.

Key signals: Source credibility, well-structured facts, clear entity data, citations.

Outcome: Your brand appears in AI-generated answers as a trusted source.

AEO

Answer Engine Optimization

Goal: Be the direct answer for a question — in Google's AI Overviews, featured snippets, or voice results.

Optimizing for: Question-answer pairs, concise structured content.

Key signals: FAQ schema, direct answers, conversational phrasing, conciseness.

Outcome: Your content is surfaced above organic results as the definitive answer.
